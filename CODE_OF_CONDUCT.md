# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project environment.

Be considerate, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, respect privacy
and licensing boundaries, and avoid personal attacks or discriminatory conduct.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior threatens contributors or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the MIYANG AI
GitHub organization. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith.
